# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Map is the new default graph view. Linked records settle into named areas, records with no links wait on a ground line underneath, and names no longer overlap. Hover a record to light its links. Trace map, Relief map and Clusters now open as Map.
- Pick a view from one switch: Map, Flow, Orbit, 3D or Freeform. In 3D, turn and tilt with small buttons instead of a dropdown.
- Files and Graph share one header, with Files and Graph side by side in the same switch.
- Fixed an error that appeared every time Freeform opened, a duplicate Center button, a full stop that could wrap onto its own line after a link, and boxy row edges in Navigate.

- Projects reopen at their files. Read two sources side by side, save a linked finding, or open Map to inspect connections.

- Signal field groups connected records in the map. Rotate the strands, pull linked records, or open a source directly from the graph.

- Atlas draws a contour map from connected records. Graph and screen changes respect reduced motion.
- Added Video edit, 3D production, and Model evaluation workspaces with linked notes and tasks. Import captions, edit lists, OBJ meshes, glTF manifests, notebooks, and JSONL datasets.
- Added Plotter and Blueprint palettes and a palette editor. Export your colors and display settings from Appearance.

- Browse community channels in the desktop app. Sign in through Groundplanes to publish, report posts, or share packages.

- Removed a missing test script from the standalone workspace page and retired two unused copies of the old model runner.
- Shortened the profile, package-sharing, and licensing screens. The README now links to a screenshot gallery and a guide to running a research project.

## 0.2.17-beta.40

- Added Spatial, a 3D view of the workspace. Atlas and Spatial support full rotation, including tilt and roll.
- Drag a record in either view to pull its neighbors. The links stay attached as the graph moves.

## 0.2.17-beta.39

- Added Clusters and Orbit. Orbit shows link distance from a record you choose.
- Added saved graph investigations. Trace a path, find the claims affected by a source edit, or compare a reviewed quotation with its current source.

## Earlier releases

The 0.2.17 betas introduced signed Windows updates, a research queue that resumes from saved passes, passage selection, and claim review. Version 0.2.16 added the installed version and channel to the update check. Version 0.2.15 added the Releases panel and preserved pending edits when opening Graph search.
